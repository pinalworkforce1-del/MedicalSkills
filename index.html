Experience the Work.

This challenge puts you into realistic healthcare workplace situations based on skills employers are asking for. It is not a certification test. It is a chance to practice judgment, experience the work, and see how your strengths may align.

Your goal: Make workplace decisions, learn what employers value, notice where you already feel comfortable, and identify skills or environments you may want to explore further.
