# BOOST Healthcare Workplace Skills Challenge — Media Manifest

The HTML is fully functional before media is added. These files replace the built-in placeholders.

## Visual style
Use the same BOOST graphic-novel/comic treatment used in the AI Workplace Challenge: wide 16:9 composition, bold ink outlines, halftone texture, realistic modern healthcare settings, diverse adult characters, no graphic medical content, and no real employer logos.

- `health_03_emr_mismatch.png` — worker comparing an EMR screen with conflicting information. Skills: detail, accountability, problem solving.

- `health_01_handoff.mp3` — “You’re at the end of your shift in a healthcare setting. A coworker taking over asks for the most important updates. Several things happened during the shift, and one item still needs follow-up. Think about communication, detail, and accountability. What would you do?”
- `health_02_schedule_change.mp3` — “A patient arrives frustrated because their appointment time changed and they were not expecting it. You can see they’re upset, and other people are waiting too. Think about compassion, communication, and problem solving. What would you do?”
